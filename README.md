# SafeStep - Accessible Navigation for Everyone

## 🎯 Our Vision
SafeStep is a navigation app specifically designed for people with mobility disabilities. While regular maps show you the shortest route, SafeStep shows you the **most accessible route** - avoiding broken sidewalks, steep stairs, and other obstacles that make daily travel difficult or impossible for wheelchair users and others with mobility challenges.

## 🌟 Why SafeStep?
Every day, people with mobility disabilities face unexpected barriers: a sidewalk that suddenly ends, stairs without ramps, or streets without usable paths. These aren't just inconveniences - they're barriers to independence, employment, education, and social life. SafeStep turns unpredictable journeys into safe, reliable routes.

## 🚀 Key Features

### 📍 **Accessible Route Planning**
- Get wheelchair-friendly routes between any two points
- Avoid stairs, steep inclines, and broken sidewalks
- Prefer routes with ramps and smooth surfaces
- See estimated travel time based on accessibility

### 🗺️ **Interactive Map Interface**
- Click directly on the map to select start and end points
- Visualize routes with color-coded accessibility levels
- See reported obstacles and warnings along your path
- Search by address or use your current location

### 📸 **Community-Powered Obstacle Reporting**
- Take photos of accessibility barriers you encounter
- AI automatically detects and categorizes obstacles
- Warn other users about broken sidewalks, missing ramps, etc.
- Build a collective map of urban accessibility

### 🔍 **Real-Time Accessibility Data**
- See which streets have good/bad accessibility
- Get detailed information about path conditions
- View community ratings and comments
- Make informed decisions about your route

### 🤝 **For Everyone**
- **Individuals**: Plan your daily commute with confidence
- **Organizations**: Help your clients/members navigate safely
- **Cities**: Get data to improve infrastructure
- **Advocacy Groups**: Document accessibility issues systematically

## 💼 The Bigger Picture
SafeStep isn't just an app - it's a movement toward more inclusive cities. By collecting data about urban accessibility, we can:
- Help cities identify and fix problem areas
- Provide valuable insights for urban planners
- Create pressure for better infrastructure
- Build community awareness about accessibility challenges

## 🎯 Our Promise
We're creating technology that understands human experience. SafeStep combines technical innovation with real empathy to solve a daily problem that most people never notice, but for some people, makes all the difference.
